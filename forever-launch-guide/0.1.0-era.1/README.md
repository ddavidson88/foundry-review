# Forever Launch Guide — Era rehearsal 0.1.0-era.1

An original, project-authored RXP guide for rehearsing the Night Elf exploration backup route on Classic Era. The route finishes with the Jasperlode Mine turn-in in Goldshire; reaching level 5 earlier does not end it. This candidate needs independent cLaunchPrep review before owner testing.

The normal starting-zone control was **32:20** at approximately **5,916 cumulative XP**. The earlier exploration run was deliberately untimed scouting, with detours and accidental deaths. It is useful route evidence, not a competing stopwatch result. Exploration remains the backup if launch congestion makes normal questing impractical.

## Scope and use

- Requires RXPGuides. This release targets **Classic Era, non-Hardcore**. Intentional death skips make it unsuitable for Hardcore. It does not claim Forever client compatibility.
- Keep Wellspring Lake, The Oracle Glade and Wellspring River, then the small death skip toward Darnassus. Omit Pools of Arlithrien. Retain Redridge and Duskwood, including the previously missed Raven Hill Cemetery.
- Pause the stopwatch for Era's extra transport from Auberdine to the Stormwind harbor-equivalent position. Resume there. Pause personal breaks consistently. Any intended discovery reached during the workaround counts once; record extra Era discoveries separately.
- Confirm discoveries manually from the game's discovery/XP evidence. A waypoint is an observed route aid, not proof that an area was discovered or XP was earned. Practice surviving the crossings; do not reproduce accidental scouting deaths.
- The second Elwynn batch and **Stonefield Farm** return come before the Stormwind errands and both book turn-ins. Then return for Fargodeep, the camping stop, Fishing and Jasperlode. Complete the mine objective before taking its planned return death skip.

The Tower of Azora book (**Archmage Theocritus' Research Journal**), Sentinel Hill book (**Rumi of Gnomeregan: The Collected Works**) and **The Great Outdoors** camping quest are Forever content. Their Era steps are manual rehearsal reminders, not promises of available items, completion or XP. The Forever profession choice from **The Woodland Protector** also cannot be validated through Era's reward behavior.

Hearthstone binding and the subsequent leveling guide remain undecided. This guide does not bind automatically or select a next guide. The owner can choose that continuation after Jasperlode.
